# The drift limit of 0.3 v_F: what it corresponds to

Generated by `tools/drift_limit.py` from the small-signal runs of the 1 um funnel on the 20 nm mesh
(`results/thz2/fine/D1_*`). No new simulation. Device area 102.5 um^2; v_F = 1e6 m/s.

The fluid model keeps the lattice temperature, a friction linear in the drift and no optical phonons.
Three estimates of where that fails, each evaluated when the largest first-order drift is 0.3 v_F.

## 1. Emission of optical phonons

A Fermi sea of fixed density drifting at beta = u / v_F is occupied up to E_F (1 - beta^2)^(3/4) / (1 - beta cos(theta)).
Its forward edge lies 2 beta E_F (1 - beta^2)^(-1/4) above its backward edge. Emission of a phonon of energy hw
from the forward edge into empty states at the backward edge opens where that spread equals hw.

| n (cm^-2) | E_F (meV) | hBN, 100 meV | graphene K, 160 meV | hBN, 180 meV | graphene Gamma, 196 meV |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1e12 | 116.7 | 0.41 | 0.61 | 0.67 | 0.71 |
| 5e11 | 82.5 | 0.55 | 0.77 | 0.82 | 0.86 |
| 2e11 | 52.2 | 0.77 | 0.93 | 0.95 | 0.96 |
| 1e11 | 36.9 | 0.9 | 0.98 | 0.99 | 0.99 |
| 5e10 | 26.1 | 0.97 | 0.99 | 1 | 1 |

At the reference density the first threshold is at 0.41 v_F; at lower density it moves up. At a temperature T the edge
is smeared by a few k_B T (26 meV at 300 K), which lowers the threshold by a comparable part of hw.

## 2. Heating of the electrons

Energy per carrier above its value at the lattice temperature: (pi^2 / 3) k_B^2 (T_e^2 - T_L^2) / E_F (degenerate gas).
Balance with a cooling time tau_E:

- whole device, heated evenly by the absorbed power P_abs: T_e^2 - T_L^2 = P_abs tau_E / [(pi^2 / 3)(k_B^2 / E_F) n A];
- a region larger than the cooling length in which the drift amplitude is beta: T_e^2 - T_L^2 = (3 / 2 pi^2)(E_F / k_B)^2 beta^2 tau_E / tau.

The cooling length is sqrt(v_F^2 tau tau_E / 2). Measured cooling time of hBN-encapsulated graphene at room
temperature: 2.5 ps at 1.7e12 cm^-2, exponential; slower and not exponential below about 200 K (Tielrooij et al.,
Nat. Nanotechnol. 13, 41 (2018)). No value is given there for 100 K; 2.5, 10 and 30 ps are shown.

## 3. Measured saturation of the drift velocity

hBN-encapsulated graphene at room temperature: saturation velocity 6e7 cm/s (0.6 v_F) at 8e11 cm^-2, falling to
2.7e7 cm/s at 4.4e12 cm^-2, attributed to the surface optical phonons of hBN above 100 meV (Yamoah et al., ACS Nano 11,
9914 (2017)). In a velocity-field law v = mu E / [1 + (mu E / v_sat)^a]^(1/a) a drift of 0.3 v_F = v_sat / 2 is
below mu E by a factor 0.50 for a = 1 and 0.87 for a = 2. That is a steady field; the fluid runs have an
alternating one, in which the heating is set by the time average.

## What follows

- 0.3 v_F is below the first phonon threshold at every density of the grid (0.41 v_F at 1e12 cm^-2), and half the
  measured saturation velocity at the reference density.
- At 300 K the electrons of the reference device are 5 to 6 % hotter than the lattice on the device average at the
  limit and up to 30 % hotter near the source contact. At 100 K they are 5 to 8 % hotter on the device average with the
  room-temperature cooling time and 20 to 28 % with 10 ps; the local bound is 50 % and a factor 2.5.
- 0.3 v_F is therefore the order of the speed at which the first corrections reach tens of per cent, and not a
  conservative bound at 100 K. The corrections named are of the same order at 0.2 v_F (heating at 100 K) and reach
  order one by 0.4 to 0.6 v_F (phonon emission, saturation).
